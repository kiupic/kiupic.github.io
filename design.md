# Task 2: Design

[Home](./) · [1. Research](research.md) · **2. Design** · [3. Plan](plan.md) · [4. Present](present.md) · [Skills](skills.md) · [Practice](practice.md)

**Time:** 8 hours (usually over several sessions) · **Marks:** 24 · **Internet:** NOT allowed (you can use your Task 1 brief)

## What you produce

**Part A**
- **At least two** annotated concept design sketches
- A **calculation**, e.g. the maximum horizontal impact force from a vehicle (see [Skills](skills.md))
- **Design notes** of about 500 words explaining how the designs meet the brief

**Part B**
- **One final, fully dimensioned CAD drawing**

Expect roughly four A3 drawing sheets and two A4 sides of calculations.

## Timing plan (8 hours)

| Time | Do this |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Re-read the brief and your Task 1 requirements table |
| 0:20–2:30 | Concept sketches: 2–3 ideas, each heavily annotated |
| 2:30–3:30 | Calculation(s), showing every step |
| 3:30–4:15 | Choose the final design using a **weighted decision matrix**. Write the design notes |
| 4:15–7:15 | Final CAD drawing (Part B) |
| 7:15–8:00 | Evaluation against the brief, plus checking the title block, dimensions and file names |

## Concept sketch checklist

- [ ] Sketched in 3D (isometric or perspective), plus key views
- [ ] Overall dimensions marked (e.g. height 1000 mm)
- [ ] Materials and finishes labelled, **with reasons**
- [ ] Sensor, light, battery and solar positions shown
- [ ] Fixing and foundation method shown
- [ ] Anti-vandal features labelled (tamper-proof fixings, no sharp edges)
- [ ] Maintenance access shown
- [ ] Each annotation links to a brief requirement

## Final CAD drawing checklist (BS 8888)

- [ ] **Title block:** name, drawing title, drawing number, scale, date, units (mm), projection symbol, material, sheet size
- [ ] **Third-angle orthographic projection:** front, side and plan views
- [ ] A **section view** through the base or fixing if it helps
- [ ] An isometric view for clarity
- [ ] **Full dimensions:** no missing or duplicated sizes, and tolerances where they matter
- [ ] Correct line types: outlines, hidden detail and centre lines
- [ ] Parts list or bill of materials if it's an assembly
- [ ] Notes covering finish (e.g. hot-dip galvanised to BS EN ISO 1461), weld symbols and fixings

## Design notes: what to cover (~500 words)

- How each concept meets the key requirements (use a table)
- Why you chose the final design (decision matrix)
- Material choice, justified with properties and costs from Task 1
- How the calculation result affected the design (e.g. wall thickness, foundation depth)
- Cost estimate against the budget
- Sustainability (recycled content, end-of-life)

## Evaluation tips (AO5b)

Go through the brief requirement by requirement: **Met / Partly met / Not met**, with evidence. Be honest about weaknesses and say how you'd fix them. Examiners reward realistic evaluation more than claiming everything is perfect.
