# Any Brief: Task 1 Research Questions

[Home](./) · [1. Research](research.md) · [Prosthetics](prosthetics.md) · **Any brief**

Use this for **any** ESP brief. Swap **[product]** for whatever you're designing (e.g. bollard, workbench, bike rack, helmet). Answer in your own words, write down the numbers, and record every source.

> **Key idea:** start with the person, not the product. Ask *"Who is going to use this, and what do they need?"*

---

## 1. The brief and the user

**Q1.** Who is the client, and what problem do they want solved?

**Q2.** Who uses the [product]? Think about age, size, ability and how many people.

**Q3.** How, where and how often will it be used? Think about indoors or outdoors, weather, vandalism and hours a day.

**Q4.** What numbers does the brief give (size, load, budget, quantity, timescale, performance)? Put them in a requirements table.

**Q5.** What are the "musts" and "shoulds"? Which one is the hardest to achieve?

**Q6.** List at least 10 pieces of information you need before designing, and say why each matters.

---

## 2. Anthropometry, ergonomics and biomechanics

**Q7.** Which body measurements matter for the [product] (e.g. stature, reach, grip, sitting height)?

- 🔍 `anthropometric data [measurement] UK adults`
- 🔍 `PeopleSize` · `NASA Human Integration Design Handbook`

**Q8.** Which percentile range should you design for (5th–95th?), and is anything adjustable?

**Q9.** What ergonomic issues apply: comfort, posture, fatigue, ease of use, visibility?

- 🔍 `[product] ergonomics` · `HSE ergonomics`

**Q10.** What forces or movements does the user put on the [product], or the [product] put on the user?

- 🔍 `[product] forces biomechanics`

---

## 3. Existing products

**Q11.** Find 2–3 similar products. What works, what doesn't, and roughly what do they cost?

- 🔍 `[product] manufacturers UK` · `[product] datasheet`

**Q12.** What features could you improve on or combine?

---

## 4. Materials

**Q13.** What materials are similar products made from, and why?

- 🔍 `[product] materials`

**Q14.** Compare at least 3 materials: strength, density, cost, corrosion/weather resistance, recyclability.

- 🔍 `[material] properties density yield strength` · `Engineering Toolbox [material]`

**Q15.** Why might the strongest material not be the best choice here?

**Q16.** Which options are more sustainable (recycled content, end-of-life)?

- 🔍 `sustainable alternatives to [material]`

---

## 5. Manufacturing

**Q17.** How could the [product] be made (casting, CNC, laser cutting, welding, moulding, 3D printing)?

- 🔍 `how is [product] manufactured`

**Q18.** Which process suits the quantity in the brief, and why?

- 🔍 `manufacturing process selection batch size`

**Q19.** What finishes or coatings are needed?

- 🔍 `[material] protective finish outdoor`

---

## 6. Technology (if the brief includes it)

**Q20.** What sensors, motors, lights or power sources could be used? Compare them on cost, power use and reliability.

- 🔍 `[sensor type] comparison` · `[component] datasheet`

**Q21.** How will it be powered, and how much energy does it need per day?

- 🔍 `solar battery sizing calculation`

---

## 7. Forces and calculations

**Q22.** What forces act on the [product] (weight, impact, wind, user loads)? Estimate their size.

- 🔍 `typical [load type] force [product]`

**Q23.** Which calculations will you need in Task 2 (force, pressure, stress, moments, factor of safety, energy, cost)?

**Q24.** What values will you need for them? Write them down now, because there's no internet in Task 2.

---

## 8. Safety, standards and regulations

**Q25.** Which British/ISO standards apply to the [product]?

- 🔍 `[product] British Standard BS EN`

**Q26.** What are the safety risks to users, and during manufacture and installation?

- 🔍 `HSE [process] hazards`

**Q27.** Which laws and regulations apply (e.g. HASAWA, COSHH, PUWER, WEEE)?

---

## 9. Cost and sustainability

**Q28.** Roughly what will the materials and parts cost per unit? Does it fit the budget?

- 🔍 `[material] price per kg UK`

**Q29.** How can waste and environmental impact be reduced?

---

## 10. Finish

**Q30.** Which 2–3 design directions best meet the brief, and why?

**Q31.** Have you checked every source: recognised organisation, relevant, contains data, cross-checked, and referenced in Harvard style?
