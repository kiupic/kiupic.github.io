## The ESP at a glance

- One realistic brief from an "employer", with **four tasks** that all build on each other.
- **90 marks** in total, worth **30% of the core grade** (A* to E).
- Window: usually **March to May**, with a resit window in **October**.
- It isn't graded on its own. Your marks add to your two core exam papers to give the core grade.

| Task | Time | Marks | What you hand in |
|---|---|---|---|
| [1. Research](research.md) | 3 hrs | 15 | Technical brief (~1,500 words), references, research notes |
| [2. Design](design.md) | 8 hrs | 24 | 2+ annotated concept sketches, force calculation, design notes (~500 words), final CAD drawing |
| [3. Plan](plan.md) | 5 hrs | 18 | 1-page programme of work / Gantt with critical path, supporting statement (~1,000 words) |
| [4. Present](present.md) | 2 hrs prep + 30 min | 24 | 20-min digital presentation + 10 min Q&A (recorded) |
| [Maths, English, Digital](skills.md) | across all tasks | 9 | Marked from everything you produce |

## Where the marks come from (assessment objectives)

| AO | What it means in plain English | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| AO1 | **Plan your approach**: be organised and cover everything the brief asks | ~13% |
| AO2 | **Use your core knowledge and skills**: materials, tech, maths, H&S, standards, applied to the brief | ~50% |
| AO3 | **Choose the right techniques and resources**: good sources, drawing conventions, justified choices | ~13% |
| AO4 | **Maths, English & digital**: accurate working, clear writing, good use of CAD/software | ~10% |
| AO5 | **Get an outcome and review it**: meet the brief and evaluate honestly how well you did | ~13% |

> **Biggest lesson:** half the marks are AO2. In every task, keep linking what you know back to the **specific requirements of the brief**. General textbook facts that aren't tied to the brief score low.

## My golden rules

1. **Read the brief three times.** Highlight every number (height, budget, quantity, lumens, timescale) and every "must". Make a requirements checklist and tick it off in every task.
2. **Task 1 feeds everything.** You can look back at your Task 1 brief in Tasks 2 and 3, but you **can't use the internet** there. Whatever you don't research in Task 1, you won't have later.
3. **Justify everything.** Don't just say *what* you chose. Say *why*, and compare it against an alternative.
4. **Show all working** in calculations: formula, values with units, assumptions, answer with units, then a sense-check.
5. **Use industry language and standards** (BS 8888 drawings, HASAWA, COSHH, PUWER, ISO 9001/14001, IP ratings).
6. **Evaluate against the brief.** Say honestly what the design does well, where it falls short, and what you'd improve.
7. **Watch the clock.** Use the timing plans on each task page.

## Rules on the day (from the sample candidate pack)

- Tasks 1–3 are done **alone, under supervision**. No sharing or discussing your work.
- **Internet:** allowed in Task 1 (Research) and Task 4 (Present prep). **Not allowed** in Task 2 (Design) or Task 3 (Plan).
- Reference **every source**. If you use AI where it's allowed, you must say so, and it may affect your mark.
- Your work is collected at the end of every session. You sign a declaration that it's your own.

> **Integrity note:** this site is for **preparing before** the assessment. Check with your tutor before using any of your own notes or websites during the real ESP. Using unapproved material can count as malpractice.
