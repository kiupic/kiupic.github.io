# Prosthetics: Task 1 Research Questions

[Home](./) · [1. Research](research.md) · [Practice](practice.md) · **Prosthetics**

A practice scenario, not a real brief. Answer each question in your own words, write down the numbers, and save your sources for the reference list.

---

## Start: the brief

1. What type of prosthesis is it, and who is it for?
2. What load, activity level, budget, quantity and timescale does the brief give?
3. What are the "musts", e.g. sustainable, adjustable, low maintenance?

---

## Types

4. What's the difference between below-knee (transtibial) and above-knee (transfemoral) prostheses?
    - 🔍 `transtibial vs transfemoral prosthesis`
5. What are the main parts of a prosthetic leg, and what does each one do?
    - 🔍 `prosthetic leg components socket pylon foot`
6. What are the pros and cons of body-powered, myoelectric and passive prosthetic hands?
    - 🔍 `body-powered vs myoelectric prosthetic hand`

## Materials

7. Which materials are used for pylons, and how do they compare on strength, weight and cost?
    - 🔍 `carbon fibre vs titanium vs aluminium prosthetic pylon`
8. What are the density, yield strength, Young's modulus and cost per kg of each?
    - 🔍 `aluminium 6061 titanium Ti-6Al-4V carbon fibre properties`
9. What are sockets and liners made from, and why?
    - 🔍 `prosthetic socket and liner materials`
10. Which materials could make the design more sustainable or recyclable?
    - 🔍 `sustainable materials prosthetics`

## Manufacturing

11. How are sockets made, and can they be 3D printed?
    - 🔍 `3D printed prosthetic socket`
12. How are metal adapters and connectors made?
    - 🔍 `pyramid adapter prosthetic CNC`
13. How do low-cost prosthetics keep costs down?
    - 🔍 `low cost prosthetics manufacturing`

## Forces and the user

14. How many times body weight goes through the leg when walking and running?
    - 🔍 `ground reaction force walking running multiple of body weight`
15. What makes a socket comfortable, and where are the pressure points?
    - 🔍 `prosthetic socket fit pressure points`
16. How is a prosthesis adjusted or aligned for the user?
    - 🔍 `prosthetic alignment pyramid adapter`
17. How heavy should a prosthesis be?
    - 🔍 `prosthetic leg weight user fatigue`

## Technology (if the brief includes it)

18. How does a myoelectric (EMG) sensor control a hand?
    - 🔍 `how myoelectric prosthetic works`
19. What grip force and motor torque do prosthetic hands typically have?
    - 🔍 `prosthetic hand grip force motor torque`
20. What battery capacity and battery life are typical?
    - 🔍 `myoelectric prosthesis battery life`

## Standards and regulations

21. What standard covers strength testing of prosthetic legs?
    - 🔍 `ISO 10328`
22. Which standards cover quality and risk management for medical devices?
    - 🔍 `ISO 13485` · `ISO 14971`
23. What's needed for materials that touch the skin?
    - 🔍 `ISO 10993 biocompatibility`
24. How is a medical device approved for sale in the UK?
    - 🔍 `UK medical devices regulations UKCA marking`

## Existing products

25. Pick 2–3 real products. What works, what doesn't, and roughly what do they cost?
    - 🔍 `Össur prosthetic feet` · `Ottobock prosthetics` · `e-NABLE prosthetic hand`
