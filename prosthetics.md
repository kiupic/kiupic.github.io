# Prosthetics: Task 1 Research Questions

[Home](./) · [1. Research](research.md) · [Practice](practice.md) · **Prosthetics**

A practice scenario, not a real brief. It combines the **Human-Centred Engineering Design** prep workbook with a prosthetics example. Answer each question in your own words, write down the numbers, and record every source.

> **Key idea:** start with the person, not the product. Ask *"Who is going to use this, and what do they need?"*

---

## 1. The user and the brief

**Q1.** Who is the user (age, size, activity level), and what do they need the prosthesis to do?

**Q2.** How will it be used, where (indoors, outdoors, wet, rough ground), and how often (hours a day)?

**Q3.** What load, budget, quantity and timescale does the brief give? What are the "musts"?

**Q4.** List at least **10 pieces of information** you need before designing, and say why each matters. Think about the user, environment, materials, forces, safety and performance.

## 8. Calculations

Show the formula, the values with units, your working, and the answer with units.

**Q29. Weight force:** a user has a mass of 82 kg. What is their weight force? (F = m × g, g = 9.81 m/s²)

<details><summary>Check answer</summary>F = 82 × 9.81 = 804.42 N</details>

**Q30. Socket pressure:** 800 N acts over a socket contact area of 0.04 m². What is the pressure? (P = F ÷ A)

<details><summary>Check answer</summary>P = 800 ÷ 0.04 = 20,000 Pa (20 kPa)</details>

**Q31. Factor of safety:** a pylon test sample fails at 3000 N and the working load is 750 N. What is the factor of safety? (FoS = failure load ÷ working load)

<details><summary>Check answer</summary>FoS = 3000 ÷ 750 = 4</details>

**Q32. Moment:** at toe-off, 450 N acts 0.25 m in front of the pylon. What is the moment? (M = F × d)

<details><summary>Check answer</summary>M = 450 × 0.25 = 112.5 Nm</details>

**Q33.** Using your answer to Q13, what is the peak load on the leg for an 82 kg user who walks and runs? What factor of safety would you aim for, and why?
