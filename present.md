# Task 4: Present

[Home](./) · [1. Research](research.md) · [2. Design](design.md) · [3. Plan](plan.md) · **4. Present** · [Skills](skills.md) · [Practice](practice.md)

**Time:** 2 hours to prepare, then 30 minutes (20 min presentation + 10 min questions) · **Marks:** 24 · **Internet:** allowed during prep · **Video recorded**

## What you do

You give a digital presentation (e.g. PowerPoint) to the client's sales team, who have a technical background. You cover the challenges of the brief, how you overcame them, and how well your design meets the requirements. Then an assessor plays the client and asks you questions.

## How it's marked

| AO | Marks | Top band looks like |
|---|---|---|
| AO1 Structure | 3 | Organised, logical and pitched at the audience |
| AO2a Content | 6 | Coherent, well justified, accurate and easy to follow |
| AO2b Communication | 6 | Clear, accurate technical information, communicated very well |
| AO3 Delivery | 3 | Delivered effectively, with reasoning justified (including in Q&A) |
| AO5a Brief | 3 | Fully addresses the brief, explains how challenges were overcome, considers alternatives |
| AO5b Evaluate | 3 | Detailed reasoning on how well the brief was met, across all tasks |

## Timing plan (2-hour prep)

| Time | Do this |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Plan the slide outline (below) |
| 0:15–1:15 | Build the slides, reusing images of your sketches, CAD and Gantt |
| 1:15–1:40 | Write speaker notes (key points only, not a script) |
| 1:40–2:00 | Rehearse once with a timer and prepare answers to likely questions |

## Slide plan (about 12–15 slides for 20 minutes)

1. Title: project, your name, the client
2. The brief: key requirements (a table or icons)
3. Research highlights: what I found and why it mattered
4. Challenge 1 (e.g. vehicle impact) and how I solved it, with the calculation
5. Challenge 2 (e.g. off-grid power) and how I solved it, with the energy budget
6. Challenge 3 (e.g. people counting or vandal resistance) and how I solved it
7. Concept designs and why I picked the final one (decision matrix)
8. Final design: CAD views
9. Materials and sustainability
10. Cost against budget
11. Manufacturing plan: Gantt and critical path
12. H&S and quality
13. **Evaluation: requirement by requirement, met / partly met / not met**
14. Improvements and alternatives I'd consider
15. Summary and questions

## Delivery tips

- **Know your timings:** about 1.5 minutes per slide. Practise until you land at 18–20 minutes.
- Speak **to the audience**, not to the slides. Use your notes as prompts only.
- Keep it to **6 lines or fewer per slide**, with big visuals. Your drawings are your best evidence.
- Use proper technical terms, but explain them briefly (it's a sales team).
- Speak clearly and loud enough for the recording. Audibility is marked under English (AO4b).

## Likely questions to prepare for

- "Why did you choose that material over X?"
- "What happens if the battery runs flat in winter?"
- "How accurate is the people counter, and how would you test it?"
- "How would you bring the cost down if we doubled the order?"
- "What's the biggest risk to the delivery timeline?"
- "How would this be maintained, and how often?"
- "What would you change if you did it again?"

**Answering technique:** answer directly, give the reason, link back to the brief, and admit limits honestly with a solution.
