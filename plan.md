# Task 3: Plan

[Home](./) · [1. Research](research.md) · [2. Design](design.md) · **3. Plan** · [4. Present](present.md) · [Skills](skills.md) · [Practice](practice.md)

**Time:** 5 hours · **Marks:** 18 · **Internet:** NOT allowed

## What you produce

- A **one-page programme of work** (Gantt chart) showing the key stages, resources, sequence of activities and the **critical path**
- A **supporting statement** of about 1,000 words covering health and safety, standards, equipment, waste management and manufacturing assumptions

## How it's marked

| AO | Marks | Top band looks like |
|---|---|---|
| AO1 Plan | 3 | Detailed, logical, follows industry practice, prioritises well |
| AO2a Knowledge | 6 | Knowledge fully explained in the supporting statement, with clear links |
| AO2b Skills | 6 | Professional notation, all skills used effectively, focused on the outcome |
| AO3 Resources | 3 | Resources and materials justified in detail, **including disposal** |

## Timing plan (5 hours)

| Time | Do this |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Read the task. Work out the numbers (e.g. 800 units ÷ 40 per week = **20 weeks** of production) |
| 0:20–1:00 | List every activity with its duration, dependencies and resources (a table) |
| 1:00–2:15 | Build the Gantt chart and mark the critical path and milestones |
| 2:15–4:30 | Write the supporting statement |
| 4:30–5:00 | Check that the timeline adds up, check the notation and proofread |

## Stages to include in the programme

1. Design sign-off and client approval *(milestone)*
2. Prototype manufacture
3. Prototype testing (impact, IP/weather, electrical, sensor accuracy)
4. Design changes and final approval *(milestone)*
5. Procurement: long-lead items first (e.g. electronics, castings)
6. Tooling and jig set-up
7. Batch production (work out the weeks from the quantity and rate)
8. Quality inspection and testing of each batch
9. Packing, storage and delivery
10. Site installation (overnight access only, so plan shifts)
11. Commissioning and handover *(milestone)*
12. Contingency or float

## Gantt and critical path checklist

- [ ] Time axis in weeks, totalling the brief's timescale (e.g. 62 weeks)
- [ ] Every activity has a start, a duration and its dependencies
- [ ] The **critical path** is highlighted (the longest chain of dependent tasks with zero float)
- [ ] Milestones shown as diamonds ◆
- [ ] A resources column (people, machines, materials)
- [ ] A key or legend
- [ ] It fits on **one A4 side**

## Supporting statement checklist (~1,000 words)

**Health and safety**
- Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974
- Risk assessments under the Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999
- COSHH (paints, galvanising, adhesives), PUWER (machines), LOLER (lifting heavy bollards)
- Manual Handling Operations Regulations, PPE and RIDDOR
- CDM 2015 for installation work on site

**Standards and quality**
- ISO 9001 (quality), ISO 14001 (environment), ISO 45001 (H&S)
- BS 8888 (drawings), BS EN 60529 (IP rating), and the vehicle barrier standard from your research
- Inspection points: first-off inspection, sampling, final test

**Equipment and processes:** list each machine or process (e.g. CNC laser cutting, MIG welding, galvanising, powder coating, PCB assembly) and **justify** it.

**Waste management**
- The waste hierarchy: reduce, reuse, recycle, recover, dispose
- Recycling metal offcuts, the WEEE Regulations for electronics, battery disposal regulations
- Packaging reduction

**Manufacturing assumptions:** state them clearly, e.g. "assumes a 5-day week, single shift, components in stock by week X".

## Common mistakes

- The Gantt doesn't add up to the timescale in the brief
- No critical path, or the wrong one
- Generic H&S text that doesn't mention **this** product's processes
- Forgetting waste disposal (an AO3 mark)
