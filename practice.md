**How to use exemplars:** read the A and the E side by side, task by task. Write down three things the A grade does that the E grade doesn't, then make sure your work does them too.

## My practice plan

| Week | Practice |
|---|---|
| 1 | Read the spec and sample candidate pack. Do the sample **Task 1** (3 hrs, timed) |
| 2 | Sample **Task 2** concept sketches + calculation |
| 3 | Sample **Task 2** final CAD drawing (timed) |
| 4 | Sample **Task 3** Gantt + supporting statement (5 hrs, timed) |
| 5 | Sample **Task 4** presentation, recorded on my phone and watched back |
| 6 | Mark my work against the marking grid. Compare it with the A-grade exemplar |
| 7–10 | Repeat with past ESPs (Summer 2025, then Summer 2026) |
